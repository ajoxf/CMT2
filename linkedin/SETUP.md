# FinCoursa LinkedIn Company Page: setup kit

Files in this folder:
- `fincoursa-logo-400.png`: page logo (400 x 400)
- `fincoursa-banner-1128x191.png`: cover image (1128 x 191, high resolution)

## 1. Create the page (about 10 minutes, from your own LinkedIn profile)
1. LinkedIn, top right: **For Business** → **Create a Company Page** → **Company**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name:** FinCoursa
   - **LinkedIn public URL:** `fincoursa` (gives linkedin.com/company/fincoursa)
   - **Website:** https://www.fincoursa.com
   - **Industry:** E-Learning Providers
   - **Organization size:** 2-10 employees
   - **Organization type:** Privately held
   - **Logo:** `fincoursa-logo-400.png`
   - **Tagline:** Practical finance and AI courses, taught by senior practitioners. CMT exam preparation, trading and AI in finance.
3. Tick the verification box and click **Create page**.
4. On the new page: **Edit page** →
   - **Header → Cover image:** `fincoursa-banner-1128x191.png`
   - **Details → Overview (About):** paste the text below
   - **Specialties** (add each): CMT exam preparation, Technical analysis, Trading education, AI in finance, Financial modelling, Risk management, Corporate training
   - **Location:** your headquarters city
   - **Buttons → Custom button:** "Visit website", linking to https://www.fincoursa.com

**About text:**

FinCoursa is an online academy for finance professionals and active traders. Our courses are built and taught by practitioners, with practice questions, worked explanations and chart-based exercises.

We start with CMT exam preparation and technical analysis, and add practical AI in Finance courses for research, reporting, risk and automation.

Teams and universities can license courses, run cohorts with live sessions, and start with a free pilot.

## 2. Add a second admin
Page → **Settings** → **Manage admins** → **Add admin** → choose a trusted colleague → role **Super admin**.
They must be connected to you on LinkedIn first.

## 3. Retire the "Team FinCoursa" profile
1. From the Team FinCoursa profile, post the text below, and tag the new page by typing @FinCoursa.
2. Change its headline to: "This account is closing. Follow FinCoursa at linkedin.com/company/fincoursa".
3. After one to two weeks: **Settings & Privacy** → **Account preferences** → **Close account**.

**Post for the old profile:**

FinCoursa now has its official LinkedIn page. Course launches, practice questions and offers will be posted there from now on, and this profile will close in two weeks.

Follow the page to keep up: https://www.linkedin.com/company/fincoursa

## 4. Connect it to FinCoursa
In FinCoursa, go to **Admin → Marketing → Settings → LinkedIn**. Fill in:
- **Company Page link:** the page's admin address, e.g. https://www.linkedin.com/company/123456789/ (the number appears in the address bar when you open the page as an admin)
- **Page name:** FinCoursa
- **Followers:** current count, optional

Save. "Copy text and open LinkedIn" on any campaign post now opens this page's composer.

## 5. Optional, once the page exists
- In **Campaign Manager**, create an ad account linked to the page.
- Then add the **Insight Tag partner ID** in the same Settings card, so ads can be measured.
