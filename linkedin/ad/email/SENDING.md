# Sending a FinCoursa email (Resend broadcast)

One address for everything: team@fincoursa.com (sender name "FinCoursa", replies come back to team@).

1. Build: ask Claude for the email (subject, preview line, body). It is built at 720px with real text and buttons,
   checked on computer and phone widths, and saved as a draft broadcast in Resend.
2. Links: every button uses a fincoursa.com/go/<code> link so clicks show in Admin -> Marketing under that campaign.
3. Test: in Resend open the draft -> Test email -> your own address. Check on phone and computer, click every button.
4. List: Resend -> Audience -> Contacts -> Add contact (or Import CSV). Put people in the campaign's segment.
   Only people who agreed to hear from FinCoursa.
5. Send: open the draft -> To = the segment (never "All Contacts" unless you mean it) -> Send now or Schedule.
6. Next batch: add new people to a new segment, Duplicate the broadcast, set To = new segment, send.
7. Results: Resend shows delivered / bounced / unsubscribed. Admin -> Marketing shows clicks, sign-ups and sales per campaign.
