## 1. Product scope: internationalised contracts vs what QFI adds

### Takeaway
Internationalised ("specified") products are a short list of about 24-35 futures and options, depending on source and date, across INE, DCE, ZCE, SHFE and, since July 2026, GFEX. Overseas traders can reach them through an OI or a direct OSP route. QFI is much broader: after the March 2025 merger and expansion it covered about 38 commodity futures, 30 commodity options, 4 index futures and 3 index options. Later additions were ETF options (Oct 2025, hedging only) and CGB treasury futures (Apr 2026, hedging only). QFI is also the only route to CFFEX.

They include soybean (No.1/No.2, meal, oil), palm olein, PTA, rapeseed oil and meal, PET resin, crude oil (SC), TSR20, bonded copper, nickel and lithium carbonate, each with matching options (e.g. DCE soybean options, ZCE PTA options) — [Orient Futures Singapore, "China's Internationalised Futures and Options: A 2026 Market Overview"](https://www.orientfutures.com.sg/china-market-access/china-internationalised-futures-options-2026/) (snippet only; host unreachable)

## 2. Exchange trading fees: are they the same for OI and QFI?

### Takeaway
Exchange fees are set per contract by exchange notice and apply to all participants. I found no evidence of a separate fee schedule or rebate for foreign or OI clients versus QFI clients. Fees change often, with frequent intraday-close surcharges or waivers.

### Cited Findings
- INE SC crude:
  - A June 2026 notice set the fee at RMB 20/lot for SC2608, effective from the 24 June night session, and made intraday close-outs fee-free — [Mondo Visione, INE fee notice, Jun 2026](https://mondovisione.com/media-and-resources/news/shanghai-international-energy-exchange-ine-has-released-its-notice-on-adjusting-2026623/); [Bitget news](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560605248911)
  - An earlier INE adjustment reportedly set crude at RMB 40/lot, with intraday close at RMB 240/lot. It is dated "March 9", but the year is unconfirmed — same search ([Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560605248911))
- INE LSFO: the June 2026 notice set the fee at 0.01‰ (0.001%) of turnover for LU2608 and for intraday close-outs on other LU contracts — [Mondo Visione, Jun 2026](https://mondovisione.com/media-and-resources/news/shanghai-international-energy-exchange-ine-has-released-its-notice-on-adjusting-2026623/)
- ZCE PTA:
  - The intraday fee for PTA2305 was set at RMB 3/lot from the 24 Mar 2023 night session. This was contract-specific and is dated — [Mondo Visione, Mar 2023](https://mondovisione.com/news/zhengzhou-commodity-exchange-notice-on-adjustments-to-transaction-fee-for-pta-f-2023323/)
  - An older baseline was RMB 4/contract — [MarketsWiki](https://www.marketswiki.com/wiki/ZCE_Pure_Terephthalic_Acid) (undated)
- DCE iron ore:
  - The DCE trading manual snippets gave a delivery fee of RMB 0.5/t and storage of RMB 0.5/t/day, but no transaction fee rate — [DCE Iron Ore Trading Manual, May 2025](http://www.dce.com.cn/dceg/file/2025-05-25/1748165227597ff8080819701ddb31019706c4744d21b2.pdf)
  - The commonly cited turnover-based fee (around 0.01%) could not be confirmed.
- Options listings: three new options were reported for 10 Sep 2026 (HRC, stainless steel, LSFO), with fees quoted per contract — [BingX flash news](https://bingx.com/en/flash-news/post/shanghai-and-ine-to-list-three-new-options-on-sept-with-fees-from-rmb-to-per-contract) (fee amounts not captured)

### Inferences
- Exchange fees are effectively identical for OI and QFI clients, since both trade under the same exchange fee notices. The cost difference lies in broker layers, FX and fixed QFI overhead.
- The OI "transfer" model adds an extra intermediary layer (OI, then the domestic FCM, then the exchange), so the client pays two broker markups. This is an inference; see section 7.

### Gaps
- No current (Nov 2026) DCE iron ore fee rate or ZCE PTA fee rate.
- No evidence found either way on foreign-participant fee rebates or market-maker discounts.

## 4. Exchange margins, price limits, delivery and holiday step-ups

### Takeaway
The exchange minimum is typically 5% of contract value in the rulebooks. In practice, exchanges set operating margins well above that and raise them often:
- around holidays (every Chinese long holiday);
- in volatile periods;
- in the delivery approach (DCE iron ore: 10% then 20%).

The same exchange margin applies to OI and QFI clients. Brokers add their own add-on on top.

### Cited Findings
- INE SC crude:
  - The minimum trading margin is 5% of contract value — [INE Crude Oil Futures Trading Handbook 2025](https://www.ine.cn/eng/market/futures/energy/sc/manual/202004/W020250806318691873930.pdf) (snippet)
  - A 2018 brief described a 5-20% range — [Clifford Chance, Mar 2018](https://financialmarketstoolkit.cliffordchance.com/content/dam/cliffordchance/briefings/2018/03/a-milestone-development-for-the-china-futures-market-crude-oil-futures-trading-on-the-shanghai-international-energy-ex.pdf)
  - INE's homepage listed an Oct 2026 notice adjusting price limits and margins for certain crude and LSFO contracts. Its terms were not retrieved — [INE](https://www.ine.cn/eng/)
  - A March 2026 report said crude position or open limits were tightened, including a 1,200-lot daily open limit — [FreshFromChina](https://freshfromchina.com/ine-trading-limits-for-crude-oil-and-energy-futures-what-changed-in-2026/)
- DCE iron ore:
  - The minimum margin is 5%. Margins tier up with open interest and rise to 10% and then 20% near delivery — [DCE Iron Ore Trading Manual 2025](http://www.dce.com.cn/dceg/file/2025-05-25/1748165227597ff8080819701ddb31019706c4744d21b2.pdf)
  - A DCE notice cut the margin from 11% to 8% and the price limit from 9% to 6%, effective from the 9 Sept settlement. It also set a 5,000-lot daily opening limit for non-FCM members and clients. The year is probably 2026, based on Mid-Autumn references, but this is not confirmed — [Mysteel](https://www.mysteel.net/news/5140028-dce-lowers-price-limits-and-margin-on-iron-ore-futures); [SteelOrbis](https://www.steelorbis.com/steel-news/latest-news/dce-adjusts-price-limit-for-iron-ore-futures-contracts-to-curb-speculation-1475675.htm)
  - Overseas access runs via the overseas broker's account under a futures-company member, which collects the margin — [DCE Iron Ore Internationalization Q&A, May 2025](http://www.dce.com.cn/dceg/file/2025-05-25/1748165186235ff8080819701ddb3329019706c3d2bb2195.pdf) (snippet)
- ZCE PTA:
  - The old baseline was a 5% minimum margin with a ±4% limit — [ZCE PTA document, 2023](https://english.czce.com.cn/en/rootfiles/2023/03/30/1681344050124601-1681344050137474.pdf)
  - In March 2026, PTA2604/2605 were raised to 12% margin with a 10% limit — [Futu news](https://news.futunn.com/en/post/69846370/the-zhengzhou-commodity-exchange-zce-issued-a-notice-adjusting-the)
  - For Labor Day 2026, PTA2605 went to 15% margin and 13% limit, and 2606-2609 to 13% and 11% — [SMM, Apr 2026](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103877378-ZCE-Adjusting-Trading-Margin-Standards-and-Price-Limit-Ranges-for-Certain-Futures-Contracts-During-the-2026-Labor-Day-Ho)
- Holiday pattern:
  - SHFE's Sept 2026 National Day notice (closure 1-7 Oct) raised limits and margins on several contracts in phases from late September. Most reverted on 8 Oct — [search summary of SHFE notice; see itiger](https://www.itiger.com/news/1134768286)
  - DCE and ZCE issued similar notices for New Year 2026 (Dec 2025), Labor Day 2026 (Apr 2026) and the Sept 2026 holiday — [Mondo Visione DCE, Dec 2025](https://mondovisione.com/media-and-resources/news/dalian-commodity-exchange-notice-on-adjustments-to-price-limits-during-2026-new-20251225/); [Mondo Visione ZCE, Apr 2026](https://mondovisione.com/news/notice-on-adjustments-to-margins-and-price-limits-of-zhengzhou-commodity-exchang-2026427/); [Mondo Visione ZCE, Sep 2026](https://mondovisione.com/news/notice-on-adjustments-to-margins-and-price-limits-of-zhengzhou-commodity-exchang-2026917/); [Mondo Visione INE holiday trading adjustments, Apr 2026](https://mondovisione.com/news/notice-of-shanghai-international-energy-exchange-on-trading-adjustments-for-the-2026427/)
- INE uses daily mark-to-market settlement of P&L, margin, fees and taxes — [INE handbook 2025](https://www.ine.cn/eng/market/futures/energy/sc/manual/202004/W020250806318691873930.pdf)

### Inferences
- Rulebook minimums (5%) understate real capital needs. Recent 2026 operating margins ran 8-15% on representative contracts, before any broker add-on.
- Margins are exchange-set and identical across routes. Route differences come from broker add-ons and from whether USD can be posted.

### Gaps

- Current Nov 2026 operating margins for SC and iron ore were not retrieved.

## 5. Margin currency, FX and RMB settlement

### Takeaway
For internationalised products, overseas clients can post USD as margin. INE applies a 5% haircut, and GFEX reportedly does the same for lithium carbonate. Settlement of P&L, fees and delivery stays in RMB. QFI investors fund in RMB, either by converting foreign currency onshore via the custodian or by bringing CNH/RMB. Since 2024 they can repatriate in foreign currency or RMB.

### Cited Findings
- Foreign traders and overseas brokers can use RMB or USD directly as margin, but USD margin "can only be used for settlement" purposes, i.e. RMB remains the settlement currency — [Capital Futures (HK), INE crude page](https://futures.e-capital.com.hk/en-US/Market/ChinaGoods1)
- INE accepts USD as margin at a 5% haircut — [INE Crude Oil Handbook 2025](https://www.ine.cn/eng/market/futures/energy/sc/manual/202004/W020250806318691873930.pdf); [INE LSFO listing circular](https://globalexchanges.com/latest-news/china-ine-circular-on-listing-and-trading-of-low-sulfur-fuel-oil-futures-contracts/118744/)
- Standard warrants used as margin carry a haircut of at least 20% — [INE Handbook 2020](https://www.ine.cn/upload/20200415/1586917782215.pdf)
- GFEX lithium carbonate: USD margin is accepted with a 5% haircut, and settlement is in yuan. This comes from a secondary source and is unconfirmed — [Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com/lithium-futures-china-gfex-overseas-traders-carbonate-2026/)
- A futures firm opening a USD margin account for overseas clients at a designated depository bank must also open a matching RMB margin account at the same branch — [INE Handbook 2025](https://www.ine.cn/eng/market/futures/energy/sc/manual/202004/W020250806318691873930.pdf)
- Under CSRC rules, overseas traders and brokers authorise futures companies with clearing qualifications to settle for them. Margin is held in the futures company's special margin account — [CSRC Interim Measures on Overseas Traders and Brokers in Specified Domestic Futures Products](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc_en/c102034/c1371293/content.shtml)
- QFI repatriation: the three-month principal lock-up was cancelled, so funds can be repatriated per investment needs. Per Aug 2024 rules, a QFI can repatriate in the foreign currency or in RMB. Registration with SAFE is made via the onshore custodian — [Baidu Baike QFI entry](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Qualified%20Foreign%20Investor/4797483) (secondary; date inconsistencies noted); [CSRC release on QFII/RQFII measures](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc_en/c102030/c1370479/content.shtml)
- QFIs were given more channels to manage FX risk — [CSRC release](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc_en/c102030/c1370479/content.shtml); [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en-hk/knowledge/publications/32e693be/china-releases-revised-rules-to-ease-investment-through-the-qfii-regime)

### Inferences
- OI clients avoid an FX conversion on initial margin by posting USD, at the cost of a 5% haircut. Losses, fees and delivery payments must still be met in RMB, so periodic USD-to-RMB conversion through the FCM's depository bank is needed.
- QFI clients convert onshore (CNY rate) through the custodian. FX spreads are bank-negotiated.

### Gaps
- No source on the FX rate or spread applied when USD margin is used for RMB settlement (CNY vs CNH fixing).
- Not confirmed whether ZCE and DCE apply the same 5% USD haircut. Likely, but unverified.

## 6. QFI fixed costs, eligibility, process and 2024-2026 changes

### Takeaway
QFI requires the following:
- a CSRC licence filed through an onshore custodian bank;
- SAFE registration via that custodian;
- an onshore futures-company account opened under CFMMC special-institution rules.

There is no investment quota (abolished 2019). Eligibility and documents were relaxed in the 2020 merged measures. An Oct 2025 CSRC "one-stop" guideline lets applicants bundle licensing, FX registration and account opening. I found no published figures for custodian fees, setup cost or a minimum AUM.

### Cited Findings
- Quotas were abolished in 2019. The New QFII Measures were issued 25 Sep 2020 and took effect 1 Nov 2020; qualification requirements were relaxed and application documents streamlined — [CSRC release](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc_en/c102030/c1370479/content.shtml); [Wikipedia QFII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qualified_Foreign_Institutional_Investor)
- Applications go to the CSRC online through a local custodian. After approval, the custodian registers the QFI with SAFE. The futures broker then opens the futures account under CFMMC rules for special institutional clients — [Orient Futures, "5 Things To Know About the QFI Scheme"](https://www.orientfutures.com.sg/china-market-access/china-qfi-scheme-5-things/); [JunHe, "What QFIs Need to Know Before Trading CGB Futures"](https://www.junhe.com/legal-updates/3016) (snippets)
- The CSRC's 27 Oct 2025 "one-stop" guideline lets applicants combine licence application, FX registration, and basic plus securities/futures account opening — [Chambers, "CSRC Unveils Work Plan to Optimize the QFI Regime"](https://chambers.com/articles/csrc-unveils-work-plan-to-optimize-the-qfi-regime); [Conventus Law](https://conventuslaw.com/report/china-capital-market-opening-up-csrc-optimizes-the-qfi-scheme/)
- A full text of the "Administrative Measures for QFII and RQFII Domestic Securities and Futures Investment" is hosted by a consultancy (Sept 2026 upload) — [Dan Young CPA PDF](https://www.danyoungcpa.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/administrative-measures-for-qfii-and-rqfii-domestic-securities-and-futures-inves-c74e1b8a.pdf)
- An older CSRC review period of 20 working days is cited for the original QFII rules. It is not confirmed for the current QFI — search summary (no reliable primary source found)

### Inferences
- QFI's fixed costs are custodian appointment and fees, legal work, ongoing CSRC/SAFE reporting, and an onshore FCM account. These make it suitable for institutions, not individuals. Individual NRIs cannot be QFIs; QFI is an institutional licence.

### Gaps
- No public custodian fee schedules (bps of AUC or per-transaction).
- No current statutory licence timeline or minimum AUM or track-record requirement found.
- Whether individuals may trade internationalised products via OI (generally yes, subject to suitability) was not verified in this pass.

## 7. OI structure: direct membership vs "transfer" via a domestic FCM

### Takeaway
INE (and by analogy the other exchanges) defines four access channels:
1. directly via a domestic FCM;
2. via an OI that routes through a domestic FCM member or an OSP;
3. via an Overseas Special Brokerage Participant (OSBP);
4. as an Overseas Special Non-Brokerage Participant (OSNBP).

Even OSPs settle through domestic members. Cost layers therefore depend on how many intermediaries sit between client and exchange.

### Cited Findings
- The four channels above are described in the INE handbook: domestic FCM as direct agent; OI working with domestic firms or OSBPs; OSBP as agent; OSNBP for own account — [INE/SHFE Crude Oil Handbook 2022](https://www.shfe.com.cn/upload/20220929/1664419499629.pdf)
- An OI is an overseas broker that does not trade on the exchange itself. It authorises a domestic futures-firm member, or an overseas broker that trades directly — [INE, "Become a Member/An OSP"](https://www.ine.cn/eng/services/members/become/)
- An OSP must be incorporated overseas and hold a home futures brokerage licence. OSP qualification is non-transferable — [INE OSP Management Rules](https://tsite.shfe.com.cn/eng/services/rules/ine/911400377.html); [INE Become a Member](https://www.ine.cn/eng/services/members/become/)
- Each member clears for its own clients and for OSPs and OIs that authorise it. Settlement and delivery run through domestic members — [INE Clearing Rules](https://www.ine.com.cn/upload/20190705/1562325696573.pdf); [Mondaq, SHFE rules internationalisation](https://www.mondaq.com/china/commoditiesderivativesstock-exchanges/1650952/internationalization-of-shfe-rules-granting-more-access-to-overseas-participants)
- On DCE, overseas brokers access iron ore under a futures company member's comprehensive account — [DCE Iron Ore Internationalization Q&A, 2025](http://www.dce.com.cn/dceg/file/2025-05-25/1748165186235ff8080819701ddb3329019706c3d2bb2195.pdf)

### Inferences
- Cheapest to most expensive (inference): direct domestic FCM account (if permitted), then OI affiliated with a mainland FCM (e.g. Orient Futures SG, owned by Orient Futures China), then an unaffiliated OI routing through a third-party FCM. The affiliated case is likely one internal markup; the unaffiliated case is two commission layers.

### Gaps
- No fee figures for OSP membership, OI filing, or carrying-broker charges.
