## 1. Regulatory framework: the Futures and Derivatives Law (FDL), access routes, account opening and KYC

### Takeaway
Foreigners can reach onshore futures by two separate routes. (a) The "internationalised"/"specified" contracts, via an Overseas Intermediary or broker. This route is open to individuals and institutions. (b) The QFI licence, which is institutional only, needs CSRC registration and has a growing product list. The FDL (effective 1 Aug 2022) reaches offshore conduct that disrupts the domestic market. Account opening needs real-name KYC, an in-person knowledge test and a trading-experience test, and identification of actual-control relationships.

### Cited Findings
- The FDL took effect on 1 August 2022 — [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/a7b035d7/latest-legislation-on-opening-up-liberalisation-of-the-prc-futures-market)
- FDL Art. 2 covers futures and derivatives activity inside mainland China. It also covers activity outside mainland China that disrupts the domestic market order and damages the legitimate rights of domestic traders, so it reaches offshore entities in some cases — [Hankun Law](https://hankunlaw.com/upload/newsAndInsights/e1b7d0974e1378c822d800d50534d929.pdf); [China Justice Observer translation](https://chinajusticeobserver.com/law/x/futures-and-derivatives-law-of-china-20220420). One English text (Lexis) appears to leave out the offshore clause and reads "within the territory" only. This looks like an incomplete translation — [LexisCN](https://www.lexiscn.com/law/law-english-1-4236467-T.html?eng=0)
- Clifford Chance's briefing on the draft warned that "a wide range of market participants may find themselves technically caught" by the extraterritorial reach — [Clifford Chance (2021 draft briefing)](https://financialmarketstoolkit.cliffordchance.com/content/dam/cliffordchance/briefings/2021/05/draft-prc-futures-law-what-to-expect-for-exchange-traded-and-otc-derivatives-trading.pdf)
- Under the FDL, foreign entities need regulatory approval to advertise or promote futures products in China. The law also sets a framework for cross-border futures trading — [China Briefing](https://www.china-briefing.com/news/china-to-tighten-regulations-on-foreign-investments-through-its-historic-futures-and-derivatives-law/)
- The CSRC's Derivatives Trading Administrative Measures drew attention for their perceived extraterritorial impact on offshore TRS structures that reference PRC assets — [Simmons & Simmons (search snippet)](https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/cmpmdgz9i00c6ut6ky1qr6aws/new-derivatives-trading-administrative-measures-trial)
- The CSRC can supervise across borders through bilateral or multilateral MoUs with overseas regulators — [AIMA](https://www.aima.org/journal/aima-journal---edition-142/article/evolvement-of-programme-trading-regulation-in-china.html)
- **Route A, internationalised contracts:** qualified overseas brokers may open accounts for overseas traders and trade specified contracts on the exchanges in their own name. No QFI registration is needed — [Katten (CSRC draft measures)](https://katten.com/China-Securities-Regulatory-Commission-Invites-Comments-on-Proposed-Rules); [Orient Futures (broker)](https://www.orientfutures.com.sg/china-market-access/internationalised-vs-qfi-china-futures-access/)
- SHFE says 6 products are open directly to overseas investors: crude oil, LSFO, TSR 20, bonded copper, SCFIS (Europe) futures, and crude oil options. It also says 32 products are QFI-accessible (date of statement unclear) — [SHFE Overview](https://www.shfe.com.cn/eng/AboutSHFE/Introduction/Overview/)
- Lithium carbonate futures and options on GFEX opened to overseas participants on 3 July 2026, the first internationalised contracts on GFEX (secondary source) — [Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com/lithium-futures-china-gfex-overseas-traders-carbonate-2026/). Orient Futures Singapore received GFEX Overseas Intermediary status — [NatLawReview press release](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/institutional-brokerage-firm-orient-futures-singapore-receives-guangzhou)
- The total count of internationalised products is unsettled across sources. FIA gave an earlier figure of 14 futures and 9 options. A March 2025 BANDS guide gave 24 futures and options on INE, DCE and ZCE — [FIA](https://www.fia.org/marketvoice/articles/china-further-expands-international-access-its-commodity-markets); [BANDS (Mar 2025)](https://bands.financial/files/BANDS%20Webinar%20-%20Trading%20Commodity%20Futures%20in%20China.pdf)
- **Route B, QFI:** the CSRC, PBOC and SAFE issued the New QFII measures in Sept 2020. From 1 Nov 2021 they covered commodity futures and options on INE, DCE, ZCE and SHFE, plus stock index options (hedging only) — [HFW](https://www.hfw.com/insights/revised-qfii-scheme-broadens-market-access-to-prc-exchange-traded-commodity-futures-and-options-contracts-feb-2023/); [YaoWang Law](https://www.yaowanglaw.com/newsinfo/11250795.html)
- QFI expansions in 2025–2026:
  - ETF options opened to QFIs for hedging only from 9 Oct 2025 — [YaoWang Law](https://www.yaowanglaw.com/newsinfo/11250795.html)
  - The CSRC announced on 21 Apr 2026 that QFIs may trade CGB futures (TS, TF, T, TL) for hedging only. CFFEX began taking applications from 24 Apr 2026 — [Caixin, 24 Apr 2026](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-04-24/china-opens-government-bond-futures-to-foreign-investors-for-hedging-102437743.html); [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/blog/china-opens-government-bond-futures-to-qualified-foreign-investors/)
  - As of May 2026, QFIs could trade 115 commodity futures, commodity options and stock-index option contracts — [YaoWang Law](https://www.yaowanglaw.com/newsinfo/11250795.html)
  - A tracker reports that on 24 Sep 2026 three exchanges added 12 futures and 11 options to the QFI list, effective 28 Sep 2026. One source says this took SHFE's QFI total to 47. Not verified against exchange notices — [futuresclock (tracker)](https://futuresclock.com/en/china-atlas/)
  - A broker reports that the merged QFII/RQFII "QFI" framework was updated from 4 Mar 2025 and that GFEX got its first QFI access. Not verified — [Orient Futures](https://www.orientfutures.com.sg/china-market-access/china-expands-qfi-access-global-traders/)
- **Knowledge test:** under the INE Eligibility Management Rules, the client must pass an exchange-recognised knowledge test at or above the exchange's published score. Individual clients, and the authorised traders of institutional clients, must sit the test in person — [INE Eligibility Rules](https://www.ine.cn/en/regulation/regulation/911319599.html)
- **Trading experience:** the 2019 rules require at least 10 trading days of simulated futures or options trading, or equivalent real trading records. The account-opening institution may waive some knowledge or experience requirements, and overseas clients may sign a commitment letter on their futures knowledge in some cases — [INE Eligibility Rules (2019 PDF)](https://www.ine.com.cn/upload/20190530/1559211977341.pdf); [CCB Futures INE guideline (2024)](https://www.ccbfutures.com/upload/20240812/20240812154255927.pdf)
- **One trading code per client:** each client needs its own trading code, applied for through the FF Member, OSBP or Overseas Intermediary. Trading on aggregated or netted multi-client positions is not allowed. In practice this rules out omnibus anonymity — [SHFE actual-control rules (Aug 2025)](https://www.shfe.com.cn/eng/services/Rules/SHFERules/202508/t20250807_828559.html)
- A Nanhua Futures executive (2026) named unfamiliarity with the onshore regulatory framework as the primary obstacle for overseas investors — [FOW](https://www.fow.com/insights/analysis-key-obstacles-and-opportunities-for-foreign-access-to-chinas-commodities-market)

### Inferences
- The QFI route is for institutions only, and its new financial-futures access (CGB futures, ETF options) is hedging-only. Foreign individuals are in practice limited to the internationalised contracts through an Overseas Intermediary.
- The in-person test requirement and the 10-day experience requirement add real friction for remote foreign individuals, though brokers can waive some items.
- The FDL's offshore reach makes offshore lookalike or swap trading on Chinese contracts a potential enforcement exposure, as well as onshore trading itself.

### Gaps
- The current (2026) INE, DCE and ZCE eligibility thresholds (test pass mark, simulated-trade count, any minimum funds) could not be fetched.
- I found no official consolidated list of all internationalised and QFI products as of Nov 2026, and counts conflict.
- I found nothing on any minimum-asset requirement for overseas individuals.

## 2. Trading rules: position limits, actual control, programme trading and HFT, abnormal trading

### Takeaway
The CSRC Futures Programme Trading Measures took effect on 9 Oct 2025, with exchange implementing rules (e.g. INE, Aug 2025). All programme traders, foreign ones included, must file through their broker before trading. HFT firms must disclose strategy types, cancellation peaks, server locations and contingency plans, and face differentiated fees. Exchanges aggregate positions across "actual control" accounts and enforce intraday opening limits with suspensions.

### Cited Findings
- The CSRC issued the Futures Programme Trading Measures (Trial) on 13 June 2025, effective 9 Oct 2025. Existing participants had to complete reporting within 6 months of that date. Clients who trade via brokers report through their futures broker — [Lexology/Chambers summaries](https://chambers.com/articles/csrc-released-the-provisions-on-futures-program-trading); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/china/commoditiesderivativesstock-exchanges/1651172/csrc-released-the-provisions-on-futures-program-trading)
- HFT is defined by behaviour (many order placements and cancellations in a short time, high daily counts). Exchanges set the numeric standards. One commentary says the working market benchmark (≥20 orders per second, on ≥20 occasions a day) has not been codified in exchange rules — [Simmons & Simmons (snippet)](https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/cmpm3fwk100c0ut6kaf1azue5/china-new-regulations-on-programme-trading); [YaoWang Law](https://yaowanglaw.com/newsinfo/8443537.html)
- HFT firms must make extra disclosures: strategy type and key elements, maximum order-cancellation frequency, highest daily cancellation count, server location, system test reports, contingency plans, and risk controls — [Chambers](https://chambers.com/articles/csrc-released-the-provisions-on-futures-program-trading)
- The rules apply to all programme trading in the futures market, including by overseas participants — [AIMA](https://www.aima.org/journal/aima-journal---edition-142/article/evolvement-of-programme-trading-regulation-in-china.html)
- Exchanges may set order and cancellation fees and trading limits, and apply differentiated fees to HFT. The INE rules say specific standards will be announced separately — [INE Program Trading Rules (Aug 2025)](https://www.shfe.com.cn/eng/services/Rules/INERules/202508/t20250814_828669.html)
- Monitoring triggers include excess orders or cancellations in a short window or a single day, and a high order-to-trade ratio — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/china/securities/1647968/csrcs-new-regulations-on-programme-trading-in-the-chinese-securities-and-futures-market)
- In Jan 2025 the five main futures exchanges said they would not cut HFT fees. Draft rules barred brokers from rebating fees or offering other benefits to flagged traders, with penalties up to licence suspension and loss of membership — [Yicai Global](https://www.yicaiglobal.com/news/chinas-futures-exchanges-wont-lower-high-frequency-trading-fees)
- Caixin (22 Aug 2024) reported:
  - Exchanges removed commission rebates for programme-executed trades earlier in 2024, so costs "soared" and HFT edge eroded. Some foreign HFT firms were considering markets such as India.
  - Some foreign firms had operated in a "grey area" through onshore goods-trading companies.
  — [Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2024-08-22/in-depth-chinas-tightening-rules-put-foreign-high-frequency-futures-traders-in-limbo-102228776.html)
- **Actual control:** SHFE aggregates the trades and positions of an actual-control account group when applying position limits, trading limits, abnormal-trading rules and large-trader reporting — [SHFE Actual Control Account Rules (Aug 2025)](https://www.shfe.com.cn/eng/services/Rules/SHFERules/202508/t20250807_828559.html)
- INE: if an Overseas Intermediary's aggregate open positions across FF Members or OSBPs reach the limit, it cannot open new positions the next trading day — [INE Risk Management Rules (Jun 2026)](https://www.ine.cn/eng/services/rules/rulebook/202606/t20260622_832207.html)
- If a client exceeds an intraday opening limit, SHFE generally suspends it, or its actual-control group, from opening new positions for at least 3 trading days. INE published revised abnormal-trading rules in June 2026 — [SHFE Abnormal Trading Rules](https://www.shfe.com.cn/eng/services/Rules/SHFERules/202508/t20250807_828558.html); [INE Abnormal Trading Rules (Jun 2026)](https://www.shfe.com.cn/eng/services/Rules/INERules/202606/t20260622_832212.html)
- Enforcement examples from 2024:
  - CFFEX: in Feb 2024 a private-fund HFT failed to report actual-control accounts and breached stock-index futures position limits.
  - CFFEX: in Jul 2024 a case involving four individuals.
  - CFFEX penalties escalate with repeat breaches.
  — [Search summary of CFFEX notices](http://www.cffex.com.cn/u/cms/www/202104/16162302d2e5.pdf)
- Under the FDL, CSRC administrative liability can include confiscation of illegal gains and a fine of up to 10 times those gains — [search summary citing JunHe](https://junhe.com/legal-updates/2239)
- Example of an exchange intraday limit (Nov 2025): GFEX capped opening on lithium carbonate at 500 lots per day for LC2601 and 2,000 lots per day for LC2602–LC2605, and adjusted fees — [Devdiscourse/Reuters](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/education/3774925-guangzhou-futures-exchange-tightens-lithium-carbonate-trading-rules)

### Inferences
- Foreign quant or HFT firms face pre-trade registration through the broker, disclosure of strategy and server location, punitive cancellation fees, and no rebates. This structurally weakens latency-sensitive strategies.
- Actual-control aggregation means foreign groups with several funds or accounts must map control relationships across all brokers and intermediaries. Undisclosed links are an active enforcement area.

### Gaps
- The specific exchange HFT numeric thresholds and fee schedules after Oct 2025 were not retrieved.
- I found no data on enforcement against a named foreign entity.

## 3. Market structure: price limits, margin and fee changes, delivery, liquidity, hours

### Takeaway
Exchanges routinely tighten price limits, margins, fees and opening caps at short notice to cool speculation (e.g. GFEX lithium carbonate and polysilicon in 2025–2026). Foreigners can take delivery only through bonded channels. Non-bonded delivery needs a PRC VAT special invoice, which offshore entities cannot issue, and QFIs are excluded from physical delivery.

### Cited Findings
- GFEX, Labor Day 2025: from settlement on 29 Apr 2025, polysilicon moved to a 9% price limit with margins of 11% speculative and 10% hedge. Lithium carbonate moved to a 10% limit with margins of 12% and 11%. Both reverted when trading resumed on 6 May 2025 — [SMM](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103297491-The-Guangzhou-Futures-Exchange-issued-a-notice-on-adjusting-the-price-limit-and-trading-margin-standards-for-futures-con)
- GFEX lithium carbonate, effective 21 Jan 2026: price limit 11%, margins 13% speculative and 12% hedge (per search summary) — [Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/education/3774925-guangzhou-futures-exchange-tightens-lithium-carbonate-trading-rules); see also [Reuters via TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2025:newsml_P8N3SU04W:0-guangzhou-exchange-to-adjust-limits-and-fees-for-industrial-silicon-lithium-carbonate-futures/)
- GFEX lithium carbonate standing rules (Jan 2026 version) include a 1,000-lot maximum order size and a 5% minimum margin — [GFEX rules](http://www.gfex.com.cn/en/RulesDRSP/202601/d432879ad8c84d01bdd36ee931f8efb3.shtml)
- Analysts note that exchange fee hikes and position limits raise squeeze risk — [Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/education/3774925-guangzhou-futures-exchange-tightens-lithium-carbonate-trading-rules)
- FIA: China's commodity exchanges are closely tied to the state, and the authorities have intervened readily in the past — [FIA](https://www.fia.org/marketvoice/articles/china-further-expands-international-access-its-commodity-markets)
- **Delivery:**
  - INE delivery warehouses sit in bonded zones, so delivery runs on bonded warehouse receipts. Tax applies only when goods leave bonded status — [KWM](https://www.kingandwood.com/hk/en/insights/latest-thinking/guidance-for-foreign-investors-in-the-shanghai-ine.html); [INE Crude Handbook 2025](https://www.ine.cn/eng/market/futures/energy/sc/manual/202004/W020250806318691873930.pdf)
  - SHFE rules bar clients who cannot provide or accept a VAT special invoice from making or taking delivery — [SHFE Delivery Rules](https://tsite.shfe.com.cn/eng/services/rules/shfe/911403538.html)
  - Offshore participants without PRC tax registration cannot issue these invoices, so duty-paid delivery is unviable for them — [Lexology/HFW](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=d28e9dc5-e3f9-41f2-9a27-9d9bcd5a3395)
  - QFIs cannot take part in physical delivery — [Dacheng](https://shanghai-en.dacheng.com/Party_2/992.html)
  - Bonded-warrant generation, transfer, cancellation and customs declaration are a learning curve for new participants — [Clifford Chance 2018](https://financialmarketstoolkit.cliffordchance.com/content/dam/cliffordchance/briefings/2018/03/a-milestone-development-for-the-china-futures-market-crude-oil-futures-trading-on-the-shanghai-international-energy-ex.pdf)
  - FOW (2026) cites delivery as the most operationally challenging aspect for overseas investors — [FOW](https://www.fow.com/insights/analysis-key-obstacles-and-opportunities-for-foreign-access-to-chinas-commodities-market)
- Trading hours: INE crude has night sessions intended partly for foreign investors, but overseas benchmarks trade nearly 24 hours (2018 source) — [Caixin 2018](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2018-04-03/foreign-investors-may-fail-to-gush-over-new-china-oil-futures-101230179.html)

### Inferences
- Margin, limit and fee changes are frequent and announced in Chinese first. Foreign desks need intraday monitoring of exchange notices and spare margin buffers.
- Domestic (non-internationalised) contracts are in practice cash-settle-or-close for foreigners, because they must exit before the delivery month.

### Gaps
- I found no source to cite for the "1-5-9" front-month liquidity concentration or for current night-session schedules (e.g. 21:00–23:00/01:00/02:30 Beijing). Both are well-known structure but unverified in this session.

## 4. Capital controls and FX

### Takeaway
All exchanges clear in RMB. Internationalised contracts accept USD margin with a haircut and allow repatriation of principal and profits through designated intermediaries. Funds that come in as RMB must leave as RMB.

### Cited Findings
- SHFE's clearing currency is RMB, and DCE states that all contracts settle in RMB — [SHFE Clearing Rules](https://www.shfe.com.cn/eng/services/Rules/SHFERules/202508/t20250807_828555.html); [DCE brochure 2025](http://www.dce.com.cn/dceg/file/2025-05-29/1748480909379ff80808197162e712440197199560431358.pdf)
- The CSRC interim measures for overseas traders and brokers created an exception to capital controls, and SAFE allows repatriation via designated intermediaries. USD collateral is valued with a haircut, cited as commonly 5% but not confirmed from a rulebook — [Lexology/HFW](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=d28e9dc5-e3f9-41f2-9a27-9d9bcd5a3395)
- QFIs and OSPs that remit foreign currency may repatriate in FX or RMB. Funds remitted in RMB must be repatriated in RMB (search summary) — [BANDS](https://bands.financial/overseas-intermediary.html)
- The State Department's 2026 Investment Climate Statement cites capital controls and a lack of policy predictability and transparency as factors lowering investor confidence — [US State Dept 2026 ICS](https://www.state.gov/reports/2026-investment-climate-statements/china)
- Caixin, Jul 2026: rising yuan volatility in 2026 is prompting exporter hedging — [Caixin](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-07-17/chinese-exporters-pile-into-currency-hedges-as-yuan-volatility-rises-102465493.html)

### Inferences
- Mark-to-market losses are settled in RMB, so a USD-funded account carries CNY/CNH conversion and basis cost plus FX risk.

### Gaps
- I did not find the current SAFE futures-specific notice text or the exact haircut.

## 5. Tax

### Takeaway
I found no authoritative current source stating whether foreign investors' futures gains are exempt from CIT or VAT, or how they are withheld. A general exemption for gains on financial products was still being lobbied for in 2019. Delivery-related VAT is a hard practical barrier.

### Cited Findings
- In 2019 ASIFMA said the QFII VAT exemption was limited and called for a general VAT exemption on foreign investors' gains from trading financial products — [ASIFMA 2019](https://www.asifma.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/20191127-asifma-cmtc-submission-on-vat-rules-for-the-financial-services-sector-eng-translated-version.pdf)
- Chinese counterparties generally do not withhold VAT for foreign investors because no withholding mechanism exists — [International Tax Review](https://www.internationaltaxreview.com/article/2a68rfy5bw2ycq1wn3h9m/china-capital-markets-open-up-new-opportunities-bring-fresh-tax-challenges)
- Adjacent regimes:
  - Stock Connect A-share gains are temporarily exempt from CIT and VAT — [ASIFMA/AAMG 2019](https://www.asifma.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/aamg-paper-on-foreign-institutional-investment-in-china-12-mar-2019.pdf)
  - The bond-interest CIT/VAT exemption for overseas institutions runs to 31 Dec 2027 — [VATupdate, May 2026](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/05/27/boosting-market-access-china-renews-vat-and-cit-exemptions-for-foreign-bond-investors/)

### Gaps
- I could not find the MOF/SAT notice on income tax for overseas traders in internationalised futures. My unverified recollection is that INE crude had a temporary exemption announced around 2018. Confirm before use.

## 7. Geopolitical, counterparty and practical barriers

### Takeaway
Sanctions exposure (China–Russia/Iran links), opaque interventions, limited institutional participation, Chinese-first notices and Beijing-time sessions are recurring barriers.

### Cited Findings
- The State Department's 2026 ICS reports that some contacts fear sanctions risk tied to China's relationships with Russia and Iran — [State Dept 2026](https://www.state.gov/reports/2026-investment-climate-statements/china)
